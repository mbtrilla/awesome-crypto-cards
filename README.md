# Awesome Crypto Cards [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge.svg)](https://awesome.re) [![Awesome Lint](https://github.com/mbtrilla/awesome-crypto-cards/actions/workflows/main.yml/badge.svg)](https://github.com/mbtrilla/awesome-crypto-cards/actions/workflows/main.yml)

> A curated list of crypto debit and credit cards.

Crypto cards let you spend Bitcoin, Ethereum or stablecoins (USDC, USDT, DAI) at any merchant that takes Visa or Mastercard. Some convert a balance held by an exchange at the moment you pay. Others charge a wallet you control. The differences between the two models (KYC, tax, FX markup, how the cashback is really paid) are bigger than the marketing suggests.

This list only tracks cards you can order today: 142 of them as of September 2026. Cards that have closed to new applicants, and cards that were announced but never issued, are left out. The same data powers [Sweepbase](https://sweepbase.net), a free comparison tool.

Each entry links to the card's page on Sweepbase and, where there is one, to the issuer's own site. The Filters section groups the cards by region, custody model and use case.
